# Phase 2 Audit – Operations & Enterprise Admin Review

Perform an additional audit focused on internal business operations, fulfillment management, staff workflows, and long-term scalability.

## Operations Portal (Separate From Admin)

Evaluate whether operations staff have a dedicated interface separate from administrative controls.

Required roles:

* Super Admin
* Admin
* Operations Manager
* Fulfillment Agent
* Customer Support
* Marketing Manager
* Finance

Verify least-privilege permissions.

Examples:

* Support cannot modify pricing.
* Marketing cannot issue refunds.
* Fulfillment cannot access financial reports.
* Operations cannot change security settings.

Flag any role escalation vulnerabilities.

---

## Artwork Review System

Audit POD artwork moderation workflows.

Required functionality:

* Pending review queue
* Approve artwork
* Reject artwork
* Request customer changes
* Internal notes
* Artwork status history
* Re-review workflow

Verify:

* Review actions are logged.
* Artwork cannot bypass approval workflows.
* Rejected designs cannot enter production.

---

## Fulfillment Operations Dashboard

Audit internal fulfillment tools.

Required:

* Fulfillment queue
* Failed fulfillment queue
* Retry fulfillment button
* Reassign fulfillment provider
* Production status tracking
* Shipping exception tracking
* Supplier outage monitoring

Verify:

* Failed jobs are visible.
* Staff can recover failed orders.
* Duplicate fulfillment submissions are prevented.

---

## Customer Service Console

Audit support workflows.

Required:

* Customer lookup
* Order lookup
* Conversation history
* Refund requests
* Replacement requests
* Shipping issue management
* Internal staff notes

Verify:

* Support actions are logged.
* Sensitive data is masked.
* Refund permissions are role-restricted.

---

## Finance & Accounting

Audit financial controls.

Required:

* Revenue dashboard
* Tax reporting
* Refund reporting
* Chargeback reporting
* Supplier cost tracking
* Gross margin reporting
* Payout reconciliation

Verify:

* Reports reconcile with Stripe.
* Refunds are tracked.
* Chargebacks generate alerts.

---

## Inventory & Supplier Management

Audit supplier operations.

Required:

* Supplier health monitoring
* Product-provider mapping
* Provider failover capability
* Supplier pricing updates
* Supplier outage alerts

Verify:

* Products can switch providers.
* Provider outages do not break checkout.
* Pricing discrepancies are detected.

## Internal Notification System

Audit business alerts.

Required alerts:

* Failed payment
* Fulfillment failure
* Chargeback
* Refund issued
* Inventory issue
* Supplier outage
* Security event
* Admin login anomaly

Verify:

* Alerts are actionable.
* Escalation rules exist.

Audit disaster recovery readiness.

Required:
