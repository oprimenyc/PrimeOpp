# Offer Engine

**Package:** primeopp-marketplace-platform

**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## States

CREATED, SENT, RECEIVED, VIEWED, COUNTERED, ACCEPTED, DECLINED, WITHDRAWN, EXPIRED, CANCELLED, CONVERTED_TO_ORDER

## Prevents

- Offer below configured floor
- Self-dealing
- Duplicate accepted offers for unique inventory
- Accepted offer after inventory sold
- Price manipulation
- Hidden fee changes
