# Recheck and Dead-Deal Detection

Rechecks configured by: deal score, urgency, inventory volatility,
retailer volatility, expiration, premium status, community reports,
historical failure rate.

Detects: price changed, coupon invalid, out of stock, wrong variant,
redirect changed, retailer page removed, membership requirement changed,
promotion expired, store inventory disappeared, shipping changed, fake
or incorrect submission.

Automatically produces correction and expiration events. Does NOT
silently leave dead deals active.
