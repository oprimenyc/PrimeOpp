# Resale Opportunity

`resale-opportunity` computes: estimated market range, recommended list
price, fast-sale price, expected fees, expected shipping, expected profit,
ROI, maximum buy price, target buy price, recommended quantity,
recommended marketplaces, confidence, missing data, recommendation.

Recommendations: STRONG_BUY, BUY, NEGOTIATE, MAYBE, PASS, RESEARCH_MORE,
DATA_INSUFFICIENT.

NEVER autonomously purchases products. Risk adjustments downgrade
recommendations when seasonal, inventory-age, variant or authenticity
risks exceed 0.5.
