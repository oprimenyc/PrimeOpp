# Content and Affiliate Compliance

- Every affiliate link has `disclosureRequired: true` and a disclosure text
  that includes the word "affiliate".
- Every publication has an `affiliateDisclosure` field (never empty).
- Every AMOS job has at least one disclosure (defaults to "This content
  contains affiliate links. PrimeOpp may earn a commission.").
- Coupon compatibility is NEVER claimed without evidence. Stack status
  preserves uncertainty (`uncertain`).
- Availability NEVER exposes fabricated quantity.
- Scarcity is NEVER fabricated.
- Historical pricing NEVER claims complete market history from sparse data.
- Resale NEVER autonomously purchases products.
- AMOS jobs NEVER include unsupported claims (prohibitedClaims computed
  from verifiedFacts; "lowest ever" / "guaranteed" trigger specific
  prohibitions).
